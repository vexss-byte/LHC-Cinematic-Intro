# Video reference analysis

This document records the visible sequence from the supplied LAPER HARDCORE cinematic reference video.

## Shot order

1. **Arena establishing shot** — dark Minecraft arena with a deep blue/cosmic atmosphere. The camera shows the stage before the title.
2. **Title reveal** — "LAPER HARDCORE" appears as the main focal point, with warm gold/yellow lettering against the dark scene.
3. **Individual member showcases** — members are introduced one at a time. The number and nickname are prominent and consistently positioned.
4. **Member information panel** — a dark information card sits in the lower-left region with warm gold edging.
5. **Bottom message/subtitle** — a short member-specific quote or description appears along the lower part of the screen.
6. **Cinematic camera movement** — wide arena framing establishes the scene, then a closer and more dramatic view emphasizes the showcased player.
7. **Individual effects** — particles, fireworks, and sound cues accent transitions and pose moments.
8. **Group finale** — the members appear together in a final lineup rather than the sequence simply stopping after the last individual.
9. **Outro** — the camera returns to an arena/title-oriented closing shot.

## Visual language

- Background: dark night/arena look with blue and cosmic tones.
- Identity accent: gold/yellow title and ordinal number.
- HUD: dark semi-transparent card with a gold border or highlight.
- Information hierarchy: member number, nickname, server identity, then member quote/description.
- Transitions should be smooth and timed to camera cuts, effects, and sound.
- The finale needs a persistent multi-member composition.

## Implementation guidance

The scene timings, stage locations, per-member copy, pose, particle profile, sound profile, and shot framing should be configurable. Client views should be synchronized to the same timeline. A viewer who joins while the intro is running should receive the current phase/state. Ending or aborting the show should restore all temporary player state.
